    Water Purifier (Acuva ArrowMAX 2.0)

    An Acuva ArrowMAX 2.0 UV-LED Water Treatment System is installed under the galley sink to purify your cold water supply using UV-LED technology before it reaches the galley tap — with no mercury bulbs, no filters to replace regularly, and no impact on water taste or pressure.

    The manual covers certifications, product specifications, step-by-step installation, indicator light status, regular maintenance, winterizing, troubleshooting, warranty, and faucet installation.

    Certifications

    The ArrowMAX 2.0 holds the following certifications:

    • NSF/ANSI-55 Class "B" and NSF/ANSI 372
    • IAPMO Certification No. W-10944 (Ultraviolet Microbiological Water Treatment System)
    • IAPMO Certification No. W-10946 (Lead Free Water Filtration Product)
    • IAPMO Certification No. W-13411 (Drinking Water Treatment Systems)
    • EPA Est. No: 98339-CAN-1

    Tip

    Pathogen Reduction Validated

    The device inactivates more than 99.999% of E. coli at 6.0 L/min, validated by SGS-CSTC Standards Technical Services.

    Info

    DC power only

    The ArrowMAX 2.0 runs on 11–16 V DC. If connecting from an AC source, an AC-to-DC power adapter is required. Do not connect directly to AC power.

    Safety Warnings and Precautions

    Warning

    DANGER — read before installing

    • Do not store in freezing conditions. Do not allow water to freeze inside the device.
    • Do not use if the device is damaged or has been dropped.
    • Do not submerge under water.
    • Do not connect directly to AC power — this device uses DC only.
    • This product is not ignition protected. Do not install in an engine compartment or any area where explosive vapours may be present.

    Info

    Installation by a qualified technician recommended

    Acuva recommends installation by a qualified technician in compliance with applicable local regulations. Installation contrary to this manual may result in property damage or personal injury.

    Installation Guide

    Info

    Flow restrictor is required

    The ArrowMAX 2.0 system requires the supplied flow restrictor (Item No. 6) to maintain a constant flow rate. Install it along the output tubing noting the flow direction arrow on the restrictor.

    Step-by-Step Installation

    1. Find an under-counter position for the ArrowMAX 2.0 and pre-filter where all tubing can be connected without being pinched or kinked.
    2. Mount the DIN-rail (see "Mounting the Unit" diagram in the manual) using two #10 wood screws, and mount the filter clip(s) (Item No. 3) for the pre-filter with the remaining screws. Note: the filter may have one or two mounting clips.
    3. Install the ½" PEX to ¼" OD Tee & shut-off valve (Item No. 1) to the cold-water line. Turn off the water supply before cutting the line. Insert ½" cold-water lines into the ½" female ends of the Tee, pushing in as far as possible; pull gently to confirm the seal is secure.
    4. Cut a length of ¼" tubing (Item No. 2) to connect the Tee to the pre-filter (Item No. 4). Connect tubing to the inlet side of the pre-filter ensuring flow direction is correct as marked on the filter. Snap the pre-filter into its wall clip(s).
    5. Cut a length of ¼" tubing to connect the pre-filter outlet to the ArrowMAX 2.0 inlet. Push both ends into place and pull gently to confirm each connection is properly sealed.
    6. Cut a length of ¼" tubing to connect the ArrowMAX 2.0 outlet to the output faucet or appliance. Install the flow restrictor (Item No. 6) at a convenient point along this tube — note the flow direction arrow. Connect to the faucet or appliance. Pull each push-fit connection gently after installation to confirm it is secure.
    7. Use Teflon tape sealant (or another appropriate sealant) on all threaded fittings and connections.

    For faucet installation instructions, see Appendix A of the manual (page 13).

    Mounting the Unit on the DIN Rail

    1. Engage the spring-loaded mounting clips on the device by hooking the clip onto the bottom edge of the DIN rail and applying slight upward pressure.
    2. As the bottom portion engages, tilt the upper hook towards the DIN rail.
    3. Release the spring mechanism once the unit is seated on the rail.

    Info

    Recommended orientation

    Mount the ArrowMAX 2.0 in the recommended orientation shown in the manual. Refer to the "Recommended / Not Recommended" orientation diagram on page 9.

    Conditioning and Powering Up

    1. With the device connected to the cold-water line, flush the unit at 4 L/min (1.05 Gallons/min) for 1 minute to ensure the unit is fully flooded. Check that all connections are tight and sealed during this flush.
    2. Connect the DC power source or AC-to-DC power adapter. Hand-tighten the nut on the threaded jack to secure it.
    3. If there is no water flow on power-up, the Acuva logo should glow green for 30 seconds (standby mode).
    4. Turn on the faucet. The logo will glow blue, indicating the UV-LEDs are operating. If the smart faucet is installed, its base will also illuminate.

    Indicator Light Status

    INDICATOR LED WATER FLOW? FAULT CONDITION ACTION
     Blue Yes No fault UV-LEDs operating normally
     Green No No fault The device is undergoing its self-cleaning protocol. This 30 second protocol occurs atstart up as well as after every 12 hours of idle time and can be interrupted to dispense water.
     Off No No fault Normal — no power required when no water is flowing
     Off Yes Unit not powered Verify power connection and that input voltage is within 11–16 V DC
     Off Yes Flow direction reversed Ensure inlet tube is connected to the "IN" fitting and outlet to "OUT"
     Off Yes Flow rate too low Ensure minimum 12 PSI line pressure. The flow sensor requires approx. 0.3 L/min (0.08 Gallons/min) to activate the system
     Red — UV-LED fault Unplug and replug the power jack. If fault persists, contact Acuva support
     Yellow blinking — Low input voltage Verify that input voltage is above 11 V DC

    Maintenance

    The ArrowMAX 2.0 is designed for years of trouble-free use. Unlike traditional UV systems, there are no mercury bulbs to replace.

    • Check monthly that none of the water connectors are leaking.
    • Replace the in-line filter (Item No. 4) after winterization, or as per the instructions printed on the filter label.

    Info

    No scaling expected

    The small quartz windows inside the unit do not reach high temperatures, unlike UV lamp systems. As a result, no significant mineral scaling or loss of UV performance over time is expected.

    Winterizing

    Warning

    Do not allow water to freeze in the device

    If the vessel will be left in freezing conditions, the system must be winterized to prevent damage.

    To winterize the ArrowMAX 2.0:

    1. Remove the pre-filter from the system and set it on its end (outlet down) to drain.
    2. Place the drained filter in a bag and store in a refrigerator or cool environment until it is time to flush and reinstall.
