    Refrigerator (Vitrifrigo DRW)

    In this post is the manual for the Vitrifrigo DRW Series Total No Frost All In One refrigerators — covering installation, electrical connection, operation, maintenance, alarms, and troubleshooting for both the DRW70A (single drawer) and DRW180A (double drawer) models.

    Warning

    This appliance uses flammable refrigerant gas (propane R290). Read this manual carefully before installation and use.

    Technical Specifications

    PARAMETER DRW70A DRW180A
    Power supply 12–24 Vdc · 100–240 Vac / 50–60 Hz (dual electronic control)
    Max consumption — normal 4 A at 12 Vdc · 8 A at 24 Vdc
    Max consumption — defrost 10.5 A at 12 Vdc (126 W) · 10.5 A at 24 Vdc (252 W) — modulating PWM
    Refrigerant R290 (propane)
    Refrigerant quantity 0.034 kg 0.032 kg
    GWP 3
    CO₂ equivalent < 0.0001 t
    Climatic class T
    Weight 38 kg 55 kg

    If using an external 12/24 Vdc power supply, refer to the defrost power values increased by 20%.

    Important Safety Warnings

    Warning

    Flammable Refrigerant — Risk of Fire

    The appliance contains flammable gas (propane R290). Do not damage the refrigerant circuit. Do not store explosive substances such as aerosol cans with flammable propellant in this appliance. The environment in which the appliance is installed must be adequately ventilated — gas leaks must not be able to reach a concentration that could cause fire or explosion.

    • Do not use electrical appliances inside the food storage compartments unless recommended by the manufacturer.
    • Keep ventilation openings free from obstruction at all times.
    • Do not use mechanical devices to accelerate defrosting other than those recommended by the manufacturer.
    • The appliance must not be cleaned with a jet of water.
    • Never try to open or repair the refrigerator yourself.
    • Before any maintenance or cleaning, disconnect the power plug.
    • Do not store explosive substances in this appliance.
    • By law, earthing of the appliance is mandatory when connected to AC supply.

    Installation

    Info

    Recess Installation Only

    DRW series refrigerators must only be installed in a built-in (recessed) configuration. After positioning, wait at least one hour before switching on to allow the refrigeration circuit to become fully efficient.

    Ventilation Requirements

    LOCATION MINIMUM DIMENSION
    Clearance on all sides of appliance 75 mm minimum
    Ventilation openings above or below (height) 75 mm minimum, width no less than appliance width
    Ventilation openings at rear (height × width) 120 mm × 120 mm minimum
    Grille opening size 5 mm maximum

    Warning

    Ventilation Is Critical

    Do not place anything over or cover the ventilation grilles. Provide two side openings in the storage unit aligned with the air ducts on the technical compartment. Ensure the recess structure does not block access to live parts (electronic board, control unit, fan) — these must be protected by appropriately sized grilles.

    Recess Installation Types

    TYPE DESCRIPTION
    Total recess (door inside) Door flush with front side of the fixing profile. Use screws already mounted in the profile and base profile supplied.
    Partial recess (door outside) Door sits proud of the front side of the fixing profile. Slide the profile and base profile in the direction of the double arrows.

    Not applicable to no-inox-panel versions. Depth dimension depends on the thickness of the front panel used.

    Fixing the Cabinet — Cell Fastening

    After positioning the refrigerator, drill holes in the cell using a 3.5 mm metal drill bit (to access drilling points, remove both drawers and the plugs from their seats). Use 4.5×45 non-countersunk chipboard screws. Cover the bushings with the supplied plugs after installation.

    Defrost Water Drain (Remote Compressor Models Only)

    On models without automatic defrost water disposal (remote refrigerant unit models), connect a drain pipe with 12 mm inner diameter to the water disposal network before fixing the cabinet in place.

    Electrical Connection

    AC Connection (Optional)

    • Voltage must not vary more than ±5% from nominal.
    • Install a differential thermal-magnetic breaker switch (IΔn=0.03 A), 6 A, type C, no further than 1.8 m from the battery/supply — must disconnect all poles and guarantee a minimum 3 mm opening.
    • Power cable must not contact any part exceeding 50°C.
    • Use of adaptors, multiple sockets, and extension cords is not recommended.
    • Earthing is mandatory by law.

    DC Battery Connection

    Connect the 12 Vdc or 24 Vdc power line directly to the battery using the cables provided. Respect polarity. Install a 25 A thermal-magnetic breaker that disconnects both battery poles. Minimum connection current capacity: 20 A.

    Battery Protection Settings (Standard)

    VOLTAGE CUT-OUT (V) CUT-IN (V)
    12 V 10.4 V 11.7 V
    24 V 22.8 V 24.2 V

    Battery Cable Sizing

    CABLE SECTION (MM²) MAX LINE LENGTH — 12 V (M) MAX LINE LENGTH — 24 V (M)
    2.5 2.5 5
    4 4 8
    6 6 12
    10 10 20

    Operation

    Control Panel Buttons

    BUTTON FUNCTION
    A On / Stand-by — hold for 2 seconds to switch on
    B Mode — hold for 3 seconds to enter mode selection; press to cycle through modes; hold 3 sec to confirm
    C Down arrow — decrease setpoint
    D Up arrow — increase setpoint

    Tip

    Capacitive Touch Screen

    The panel uses a capacitive touch screen — the lightest touch of a finger is sufficient. It does not respond to inanimate objects, nails, or gloves. Clean the keypad with a damp cloth to maintain button efficiency. The keypad automatically locks after 60 seconds — press any button for 2 seconds to unlock.

    Tip

    Motor Protection Delay

    The compressor will only start approximately 3 minutes after the last power-on. This applies after every power interruption, including blackouts. For best performance, switch the refrigerator on at least 6 hours before putting food in.

    Operating Modes — DRW180A (Double Drawer)

    TOTAL FRIDGE

    +4°C / +4°C

    Both drawers in fridge mode

    FRIDGE / FREEZER

    +4°C / −18°C

    Upper = fridge, lower = freezer

    TOTAL FREEZER

    −18°C / −18°C

    Both drawers in freezer mode

    Operating Modes — DRW70A (Single Drawer)

    TOTAL FRIDGE

    +4°C

    Drawer in fridge mode

    TOTAL FREEZER

    −18°C

    Drawer in freezer mode

    To keep food at −18°C in the DRW180A, use the lower drawer. Switching from Total Fridge to Total Freezer can take 8–12 hours to reach the new temperature.

    Adjustable Temperature Ranges

    MODE ADJUSTABLE RANGE
    Fridge +2°C to +8°C
    Freezer −20°C to −15°C

    The lower the temperature setting, the higher the unit's power consumption.

    Anti-Mould Function (Storage / Non-Use)

    When the unit will not be used for an extended period: turn off and empty the refrigerator, open the upper drawer, and extract the ventilation mechanism in the centre of the lid (press both buttons and pull until it clicks into the locked position). Close the drawer gently so it remains slightly ajar (approximately 1 cm). To return to normal operation, open the drawer by the main handle and reinsert the mechanism by pressing the two buttons until it locks at the end of its travel.

    Maintenance

    AREA CLEANING METHOD
    Exterior Sponge or soft cloth only. Do not use solvents or abrasive products.
    Interior Warm water with bicarbonate of soda or vinegar. Rinse and dry with a soft cloth. No abrasive products.
    Extended non-use Remove plug, empty the refrigerator, clean the interior, and leave the door ajar to prevent mould and odours.

    Removing the Drawers

    • Upper drawer: Disconnect the spring cable (use force on the connector and rotate the ring nut 90° anti-clockwise), then press the levers and remove the drawer.
    • Lower drawer: Press the side levers — no connector to release.

    Replacing the Upper Drawer LED Light (DRW180A)

    Warning

    Disconnect Power Before Replacing LED

    Before replacing the LED light, ensure the refrigerator is disconnected from all electrical supply. LED replacement must be done by a suitably qualified person.

    1. Remove the protective cover perpendicular to the base — take care not to damage the fixing pins.
    2. Disconnect the light's power cable.
    3. Replace the LED light profile.
    4. Reconnect the power cable.
    5. Replace the cover.

    Alarm Codes

    Alarms appear on the display alternating with the setpoint every 2 seconds, accompanied by an audible alarm. Press any button to silence the alarm sound at any time.

    DRW70A Alarm Codes

    CODE DESCRIPTION EFFECT ACTION
    E1 Drawer sensor error Continues in recovery mode temporarily Remove food, switch off, call service immediately
    E3 Evaporator sensor error Continues in recovery mode temporarily Remove food, switch off, call service immediately
    E4 External sensor error Information only — operates normally Report to service centre
    door Drawer open >1 minute Cooling fans stop — food may perish Close drawer; if persists, contact service
    A1 Drawer high temperature Information only — operates normally Check for heat sources inside the compartment
    A3 Evaporator high temperature Information only — operates normally If persists, contact service
    EdF Long defrost alarm Unit may not cool optimally If persists, contact service
    Link Control panel not communicating with main board Information only If persists, contact service
    batt 12v / batt 24v Battery pre-alarm — voltage below warning level Information only — operates normally Monitor power supply
    A-batt 12v / A-batt 24v Battery alarm — voltage below minimum safety level Refrigerator switches off to protect battery Wait for voltage to recover above minimum level

    DRW180A Additional Alarm Codes

    CODE DESCRIPTION ACTION
    E1 Lower drawer sensor error Remove food, switch off, call service immediately
    E2 Upper drawer sensor error Remove food, switch off, call service immediately
    door1 Lower drawer open >1 minute Close drawer; if persists, contact service
    door2 Upper drawer open >1 minute Close drawer; if persists, contact service
    A1 Lower compartment high temperature Check for heat sources in lower compartment
    A2 Upper compartment high temperature Check for heat sources in upper compartment

    Troubleshooting

    PROBLEM POSSIBLE CAUSE SOLUTION
    Unit does not work / temperature too high Plug not properly connected; temperature not set correctly; near heat source or direct sunlight; insufficient clearance; refrigerator too full Check plug; set lower temperature; reposition; ensure 75 mm clearance; reduce load
    Cools excessively Temperature setting too low Set a higher temperature
    Front frame is hot Normal — insulated pipes on front frame prevent condensation on drawer seal Not a fault — no action required
    Unpleasant smell Spoilt food; food with strong smell Clean refrigerator; wrap strong-smelling foods in film
    Frost on inside walls Drawer not fully closed; food blocking ventilation openings Ensure drawer closes fully; clear ventilation openings
    Condensation on inside walls Drawer left open; food with high moisture content Keep drawer closed; wrap moist food in film
    Refrigerator does not stop Internal temperature too high; needs reset Check drawers close tightly; disconnect power for 1 minute and reconnect
    Courtesy light does not come on Light faulty Call the service centre
    Noisy operation Not sitting flat; in contact with vibration-causing object Level the unit; ensure no contact with vibrating objects
