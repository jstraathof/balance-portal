    Guided Tours

    City centre, Bo-Kaap, and township tours are best done with a reputable local guide. For self-drive Peninsula and Winelands itineraries, see the Visiting South Africa guide.

    Safaris

    If you're in Cape Town for your boat's splash, the commissioning team needs approximately three weeks to fully commission your vessel before you can take delivery. That window is the perfect time to head out on safari — either a day trip from Cape Town, or a multi-day stay at one of South Africa's incredible reserves. Contact Letitia to arrange it.

    Both reserves below are approximately two hours from the city — a full-day Big Five experience with an early start. Contact Letitia for bookings and current availability.

    Concierge Service

    For owners visiting Cape Town, BCCT has a longstanding relationship with Highline Tours & Travel, a travel specialist with an excellent track record looking after Balance customers. Letitia Muller is your dedicated contact and the best starting point for anything travel-related during your visit.

    You're always free to book things independently — but Letitia's real value is in putting together a proper bespoke itinerary tailored to your schedule: accommodation, day tours, safari, activities, airport transfers, and everything in between, all coordinated so you don't have to think about the logistics. Whether you're here for a week or three, it's worth a conversation.

    For the Winelands, the Garden Route, and extended South African travel, see the Visiting South Africa guide — Letitia can help with those too.
