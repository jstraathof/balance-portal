    Where to Stay

    V&A Waterfront

    The V&A Waterfront is the base we recommend without hesitation. It's a short drive from the Two Oceans Marine facility, and once your boat is launched she'll be berthed in the marina — meaning you can walk down to the dock whenever you like. The Waterfront is widely regarded as one of the safest parts of Cape Town, with excellent restaurants, great shopping, and easy access to the rest of the city.

    V&A Waterfront Apartments

    Many Balance owners opt for a self-catering apartment for longer stays — particularly during the handover and departure visit. These apartments overlook the marina where your boat will be berthed after launch, which makes moving aboard when commissioning is complete considerably easier.     Atlantic Seaboard

    Sea Point and Greenpoint on the Atlantic Seaboard are a 10-minute Uber from the Waterfront and offer excellent restaurants and a more residential feel. Clifton and Camps Bay suit beach-focused stays, though they're a slightly longer commute to the yard. All of these areas are well-regarded for safety and convenience.

    Tip

    Load shedding (scheduled power cuts) is a reality in South Africa. Before confirming any hotel or apartment, check that it has a generator or inverter. Most of the hotels listed have generators. For apartments, always ask before booking — it makes a real difference in the evenings.

    Getting Around

    Hiring a car is strongly recommended, particularly if you plan to visit the Cape Peninsula or areas outside the city centre. All major international rental providers are represented at Cape Town International Airport. South Africans drive on the left.

    Uber operates reliably in Cape Town and is the recommended option for evenings out. Stick to certified ride-hailing services rather than metered taxis. Fares are very reasonable by international standards.

    Cape Town International Airport is approximately 25–30 minutes from the V&A Waterfront. Uber or a pre-booked airport transfer is recommended. Allow extra time during weekday peak hours.

    Safety

    Cape Town is a wonderful city and the areas around the V&A Waterfront and Atlantic Seaboard are safe, well-patrolled, and busy day and night. As in any major city, stay sensible with your belongings — particularly in the CBD and inner city. Stick to Uber after dark, and don't leave valuables visible in a parked car. Our recommendations in this guide are all within the safe, well-visited parts of the city.
