    Country Info

    Entry Requirements

    Most passport holders receive a free 90-day visitor's visa on arrival at Cape Town International Airport. Ensure your passport has at least 30 days' validity beyond your intended departure date and at least one blank page for entry stamps. Confirm current requirements with the South African Department of Home Affairs or your nearest consulate before travelling.

    Currency & Payment

    South Africa uses the Rand (ZAR). Major international credit cards are widely accepted. ATMs are readily available at the Waterfront and airport. The Rand's exchange rate is generally favourable for USD, EUR, and GBP — making Cape Town a genuinely good place to purchase gear, equipment, and provisions for your boat.

    Healthcare

    South Africa's private healthcare system is world-class. Should you need medical attention during your visit, you will be treated by excellent doctors in outstanding private hospitals. Cape Town is, after all, where the world's first successful heart transplant was performed. Travel insurance that covers private medical care is recommended. If you need a referral to a GP during your visit, contact Tamara and she'll point you in the right direction.
