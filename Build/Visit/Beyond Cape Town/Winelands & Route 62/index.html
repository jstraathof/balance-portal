    Winelands & Route 62

    World-class wine, food, and scenery. Could keep you busy for weeks.

    Winelands

    The Cape Winelands covers about 21,000 square kilometres. There are hundreds of picturesque wine farms to visit, taste, stay at, and engage with. I've dedicated a whole separate page to the Winelands on this portal, but here's a quick map of the key sub-districts:

    Stellenbosch

    The heart of it all. Second oldest town in SA, student energy, widest selection of famous wines. Worth staying a night or two.

    Franschhoek

    The "French corner." Huguenot heritage, beautiful valley, some of the best restaurants in the country.

    Paarl

    Packed with variety: cheese farms, distilleries, breweries, cheetah sanctuary, quad bikes, and hidden farms in the mountain pass.

    The Swartland

    The lesser-visited northern stretch. Drier, more rustic, bordering the Cederberg. Increasingly interesting for wine.

    The Helderberg

    Somerset West and surrounds. Beautiful mountains, iconic views of False Bay, historic wine farms and olive oil tasting.

    Route 62

    As you drive north-east through the Cape mountains, the Winelands stretch further into the river valleys deep into the Western Cape. Starting from Robertson, the R62 road runs all the way to Humansdorp near Cape St Francis, where the Balance 526 and 580 are built. It's one of the great South African road trips.

    Along the way: quaint villages like Montagu and Barrydale, farm guesthouses, wineries, hot springs, art galleries, and roadside pubs. Ronnies Sex Shop is actually a pub. Go on in.

    Oudtshoorn is an interesting stop surrounded by wine, ostrich ranches, and the Cango Caves, Africa's largest and most spectacular show cave. From here it's also a short drive into the Garden Route.
