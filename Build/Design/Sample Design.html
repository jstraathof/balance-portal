    Sample Design

    Tiger Lily is a Balance 502 owned by Phil Berman, president of Balance Catamarans USA. Here are the exact design choices Phil made — a warm, rich interior pairing Walnut cabinetry with Italian Oak floors and Natural Hopsack upholstery. Tiger Lily is also the first Balance to feature Chilewich cockpit flooring — a material we're very enthusiastic about. The range of Chilewich patterns and colorways is extensive, and we highly recommend it as a cockpit floor option.

    Balance 502

    Tiger Lily

    Owner: Phil Berman, President — Balance Catamarans USA

    Cabinetry Veneer Concepts Walnut
    Countertop Avonite Ice White 8064
    Salon Floor Aspen Italian Oak
    Headboard Romo Veranda Horizon Silver Birch M673/03
    Salon Fabric Romo Veranda Hopsack Natural M608/02
    Cockpit Fabric Sunbrella Revive Pewter 11500-0004
    Cockpit Floor Chilewich Boucle Natural ★ First on a Balance

    Info

    Chilewich is a premium woven vinyl flooring brand with a huge range of patterns, textures, and colorways — all designed for durability in marine environments. Tiger Lily is the first Balance to use it as a cockpit floor, and we love the result.
